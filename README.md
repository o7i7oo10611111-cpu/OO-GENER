## Credits & Origins / 致謝與原始架構

**Built on top of SNARE GEN by @a200xeaf (@000formforum), experimental re-engineered with custom visual routing.**

All core snare synthesis architecture and original sample banks belong to **@a200xeaf (@000formforum)**. This experimental edition expands upon it with autonomous rhythm generation, 40 custom WAV audio slices, and enhanced variation and chaos effects.

所有核心小鼓合成架構與原始取樣庫皆源自 **@a200xeaf (@000formforum)** 的 **SNARE GEN**。本實驗改編版於其基礎上擴充了節奏生成及加入了40個WAV音訊切片，並在變化及混亂上的效果有所增強。

---

## Download & Install

1. Download **`OO_GENER_v1.0.zip`** from **Releases** and unzip it.
2. Drag **`formforum - SNARES - SNARE GEN (OO GENER Edition).amxd`** onto any **MIDI Track** in Ableton Live.
3. Enable **`BEAT GEN ON`** and press **Play**.

---

## 下載與安裝

1. 前往 **Releases** 下載 **`OO_GENER_v1.0.zip`** 並解壓縮。
2. 將 **`formforum - SNARES - SNARE GEN (OO GENER Edition).amxd`** 拖進 Ableton Live 的任意 **MIDI 軌**。
3. 確認 **`BEAT GEN ON`** 為開啟狀態，按下 **Play（空白鍵）** 即可開始生成。
